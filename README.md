# Financial Performance Analysis — SQL Portfolio Project

## Overview
SQL-based analysis of major public companies' financial statements (2009–2023), covering profitability trends, valuation metrics, leverage ratios, and cash flow dynamics across sectors.

## Business Questions
1. Which sectors show the highest and most stable EBITDA margins over time?
2. How do companies rank by profitability within their sector?
3. Which companies show abnormal revenue growth compared to their sector average?
4. How has Net Income and Free Cash Flow evolved year-over-year per company?
5. What is the relationship between Debt/Equity ratio and ROE across sectors?

## Dataset
- **Source**: Kaggle — Financial Statements of Major Companies (2009–2023)
- **Author**: Rishabh Patil
- **License**: Database Contents License (DbCL)
- **Coverage**: Multi-sector public companies, annual financials

## Technical Skills Demonstrated
- Window Functions (RANK, DENSE_RANK, LAG, AVG OVER, NTILE)
- Common Table Expressions (CTEs) — single and multi-CTE
- JOIN across multiple tables
- CASE WHEN for segmentation and labeling
- NULL handling (COALESCE, NULLIF, IS NULL)
- Data cleaning and standardization
